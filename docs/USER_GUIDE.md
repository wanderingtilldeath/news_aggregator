# Postpaper: a navigation guide

How to use it:

- Want to find one page? Go to **section 4, "Every page, A to Z"**.
- Cannot find a page like *Manage your subscription*? Go to **section 5, "Pages with no menu link"**.
- Want to do one thing (switch to weekly email, print a PDF, delete your data)? Go to **section 7, "I want to…"**.
- Never seen the site? Read section 1, then section 2, then try the walk in section 12.

A "click path" in this guide is written like this: **Top bar → More → Read → Archive**. It means: click *More* in the bar at the top, then *Read*, then *Archive*.

---

## 1. What Postpaper is, in one minute

Postpaper is a **daily news briefing**. It reads the news from about fifty Indian and international publishers a few times a day, picks the stories that matter most, and gives you a short summary of each with a plain line on **why it matters**. You can read the whole day in about five minutes.

- It is built first for **UPSC civil services exam** readers and for **technology** readers (the *UPSC* and *Engineering* editions), but anyone can use it.
- **Publishers write the news; Postpaper summarises it** and always links to the original.
- **The summaries are written by an AI** and can be wrong. Check anything important in the original story.
- **Reading is free.** No account, no cookie banner, no ads. The daily email is free. A paid plan (Pro) is optional.

## 2. The three places that hold every link

Almost everything on the site can be reached from one of three places. Learn these and you will not get lost.

### 2.1 The bar at the top (it stays there as you scroll)

```
[ logo ]  Top stories · India · World · UPSC · Tech · AI · Startups   [Play ▾] [More ▾] [🔍 Search] [☾] [Notepad] [Log in]
```

| Item | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Logo** | The home page |
| **Top stories, India, World, UPSC, Tech, AI** | The home page filtered to one section |
| **Startups** | The startup funding page |
| **Play** | A menu with the six daily games, and "All games" |
| **More** | A menu of study, reading and explore pages (the full list is below) |
| **Search** | Type a word and press Enter. Press the **/** key on any page to jump into it |
| **Sun or moon icon** | Switches between light and dark mode |
| **Notepad** | The sentences you saved from stories, and notes you write yourself |
| **Log in** | The log-in page. Once you are logged in it becomes your **username**, which opens the account menu (2.3) |

On a phone the same items sit in two rows instead of one.

**The More menu has three groups:**

- **Study:** Quiz, Flashcards, Concepts, Bookmarks.
- **Read:** Archive, Weekly revision, Week ahead, Monthly compilations, Editorials, E-paper, Underreported.
- **Explore:** Who is in the news, Slack and Discord.

### 2.2 The footer (the bottom of every page)

The footer lists **almost every public page**, in three columns. If a page is not in the bar, look here first.

- **Read:** UPSC edition, Engineering edition, Archive, Weekly revision, Monthly compilations, Editorials, E-paper, Underreported, Startup funding.
- **Study:** Quiz, Flashcards, Games, Bookmarks, Notepad, Concepts, Week ahead, Who is in the news.
- **About:** Get it by email, Log in, **Pro**, Slack and Discord, Contact and support, Sources and takedown, Privacy, Terms.

Two pages are reachable **only** from the footer: **Contact and support** and **Pro**. (Pro is also reachable from inside your account.) The **UPSC edition** and **Engineering edition** links are the only way to switch editions from the menus.

### 2.3 The account menu (only after you log in)

Click your username in the bar. You get: **Your account**, **Membership**, **Sign-in and security**, **Admin dashboard** (only for the site's admins), and **Log out**.

Inside the account area there is also a side menu: **Overview, Membership, Sign-in and security, Notepad, Saved stories, Daily quiz**, and on the Overview page a row of tiles: **Notepad, Saved stories, Daily quiz, Flashcards, E-paper**.

### Two floating buttons

As you scroll down a long page, a **Back to top** arrow and a **Subscribe** button appear in the corner.

## 5. Pages with no menu link (and exactly how to reach them)

These pages carry a private key in the address (called a **token**). That is why they are not in any menu: the address itself is the proof that you are the right person. **Do not forward these emails or share these links.**

### 5.1 Manage your subscription  ← the one most people look for

This page lets you change the **reading length**, **how often** the email comes, the **topics** you follow, **unsubscribe**, and **delete your data**.

There is **no "Manage subscription" button in the menus or footer.** Use either of these:

1. **From any Postpaper email.** Scroll to the bottom and click **Manage your subscription**.
2. **From the website, if you do not have an email handy:**
   **Top bar → Log in → scroll down to "Newsletter subscribers" → type the address you subscribed with → click "Email me a link".**
   Postpaper emails you a fresh link to your manage page. It arrives within a minute or two (check spam). You can ask again after a couple of minutes. The site gives the same answer whether or not the address is subscribed.

If you type `/manage` into the address bar without the long token, you will see "This link is incomplete". That is expected.

On the manage page you will see: your address (partly hidden, like `a***@example.com`), **Reading length**, **How often**, **Topics to follow**, a **Save changes** button, an **Unsubscribe instead** link, and at the bottom **Delete my data** (two clicks).

### 5.2 Confirm your email (newsletter)

After you subscribe, we email you a confirmation link. Open it and the page confirms your address. Until then you receive nothing. If it does not arrive, subscribe again from the home page.

### 5.3 Unsubscribe

Every email has an **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom. It opens a page with a button, so email scanners cannot unsubscribe you by accident. You can also use Manage → **Unsubscribe instead**.

### 5.4 Delete my data (newsletter)

Inside the manage page, at the very bottom. It erases your address, your choices and the record of what we sent you. (Unsubscribing only stops the email; we keep your address marked "unsubscribed".)

### 5.5 Reset your password

**Log in → Forgot your password? →** enter your email → open the link we send. The link works once and lasts one hour. It only works if the address on your account was confirmed. If your account has no email, use **Log in → Use a recovery code**.

### 5.6 Confirm the email on your account

On **Account → Sign-in and security** you can add an email. We mail you a link; opening it lands on "Confirm your email" and the address becomes active for log-in and resets.

### 5.7 Pro printable edition

Open **Account menu → Membership → Printable edition** (visible when your Pro is active). It is also linked at the end of Pro emails.

### 5.8 Stop the Slack or Discord posts

Every post we make in your channel ends with **Stop these posts**. Click it, then press the button.

### 5.9 Admin pages

**Admin** is only for the site's owner and has no link for visitors, except a small **Admin sign-in** link at the very bottom of the Log in page. You need an account the owner has marked as an admin and then a shared admin password.

### 5.10 Pages you only reach by clicking through

- **A day in the Archive** and **a month in Monthly compilations**: click inside those pages.
- **A person or place page**: click a name on *Who is in the news*, or a name chip on a story page.

**What is not on the website at all:** the **Telegram channels and bot** (if the owner has switched them on) live inside Telegram, with no link on the site. The bot answers `/today`, `/topic <word>`, `/quiz`, `/brief` and `/help`.

## 8. What is on the main pages

### 8.1 The home page, top to bottom

1. **Date, logo, weather, and "Get it free by email".**
2. **The sections bar** (section 2.1).
3. **The front page:** three columns that end on the same line. Left: stories as text. Middle: the **lead story** and three more beside thumbnails. Right: **Top stories** (numbered) and **Latest**.
4. **Today in 60 seconds:** a short paragraph summing up the day.
5. **A dark band** of four more stories ("Also today").
6. **Concepts of the day** and **Who is in the news.**
7. **Startup funding this week** (the five newest rounds, with a link to all).
8. **Today in history:** three entries from Wikipedia for today's date, with a credit. It shows only on days the data was fetched.
9. **More top stories**, with the **Brief / Detailed** switch.
10. **A row per section** (India, World, and so on) with its remaining stories and a "See all" link.
11. **The signup box** at the very end.

A story is shown **once** on the front page. If several outlets reported the same event you see one card with **"+2 more outlets"**, and the others are listed on its story page.

### 8.2 A story page, top to bottom

1. Headline, publisher, how long ago, reading time, a **Key story** label if the AI scored it 4 or 5 out of 5.
2. Tools: **A− / A+ / Narrow / Wide** and a **bookmark** button.
3. The picture.
4. **Why it matters** (the red box).
5. **Background, Who it affects, What to watch** (when the AI had something to say; "What to watch" shows a date when the article gave one).
6. **The short version:** summary bullets, a **Read aloud** button, and a save-to-Notepad button on each bullet.
7. **At a glance:** chips with the figures in the story (amounts, percentages, counts).
8. **Names in the story** (people, organisations, places).
9. **Concepts in this story:** short explanations of terms used.
10. **Also covered by** or **The story so far** (a timeline when coverage spans several days).
11. **Exam lens**, only for top UPSC-type stories: relevance to Prelims, Mains, Essay and Ethics; dimensions; arguments for and against; exact facts to remember; a three-point answer skeleton. Marked as AI-generated.
12. **Share** buttons, then **Read the full article** at the publisher.
13. **More in this section.**

### 8.3 Study pages

- **Quiz:** five questions from today's stories, one at a time, with an explanation after each answer; a score and recap at the end; a tab for each edition.
- **Flashcards:** question-and-answer cards from facts in the top stories. Cards you miss return soon, cards you know return later (spaced repetition).
- **Concepts:** a glossary, with A–Z jump links.
- **Bookmarks / Notepad:** your saved items. Free: kept in your browser. Logged in: bookmarks follow your account. **Notepad:** anyone can write a note of their own (the box at the top), and use **Edit** under any saved sentence to reword it or add a comment. **Pro** adds sync across devices, tags and search.

### 8.4 Reading pages

- **Archive:** a list of past days; click one for that day's ranked stories.
- **Weekly revision:** the last seven days ranked, made to print.
- **Monthly compilations:** the month's key stories grouped by UPSC syllabus area, to print. The current month grows each day.
- **Editorials:** The Hindu and The Indian Express side by side, each with a summary, why it matters, and exam relevance.
- **E-paper:** today's edition as a printed-paper layout.
- **Underreported:** important stories from the last two days that only one source carried.
- **Week ahead:** the votes, deadlines, meetings and launches the news names for the next two weeks, grouped by day. Dates are read by the AI and can be wrong.
- **Who is in the news:** a map of the names mentioned most; lines join names that appear together. Click one for its stories.
- **Startups:** funding rounds of the last week with amount, stage and investors. A round several outlets covered is listed once.

### 8.5 Games

Six puzzles, new every day and the same for everybody: **Daily Wordle**, **Headline blank** (a real headline with one word missing), **Spelling Bee**, **Word ladder**, **Country guess** (name a country from its outline), **Who is it?** Scores stay in your browser.

## 9. Email, channels, accounts and Pro in short

### The email

Subscribe from the home page and confirm your address. You choose: **reading length** (1-minute or 7-minute), **how often** (every morning, or once a week on Sunday), and optional **topics** (up to five words, such as "monsoon, rbi", which get their own section). The email opens with *Today in 60 seconds*, then top stories and a short section per category. Every headline opens its story page on Postpaper. The footer has Unsubscribe, Manage your subscription and the daily/weekly switch. An address that is never confirmed is deleted after 30 days.

### Slack, Discord and Telegram

- **Slack or Discord:** on the Slack and Discord page, paste the channel's webhook address and choose an edition. A test message arrives at once, then five stories each morning. Every post has a **Stop these posts** link. If the channel keeps refusing posts for three days in a row, Postpaper disconnects it.
- **Telegram:** posts to channels, and a bot you can message (if switched on). No link on the website.

### Accounts (optional)

A free account needs a **username and password**; an email is optional. You are shown **ten recovery codes** once: save them. Logged in, your **bookmarks, flashcard progress, streak and missed quiz questions** follow you to every device. You can also add a confirmed email (log in with it, reset by email), sign in with Google (if the owner switched it on), change your password, log out everywhere, and delete the account.

### Pro

Reading, the quiz, flashcards and your streak are free for everyone. **Pro** adds: no sponsor line in your email, a printable edition every day and a printable week, being **sent first** on busy days, and a **synced notepad** with notes, tags and search. You pay through Razorpay (UPI, card, net banking); Postpaper never sees card details. Use the **same email address** you subscribe with. One payment buys one period (the default plans are about a month and about three months) and **does not renew by itself**. You get a reminder three days before it ends and a note when you renew. The buy buttons appear on the Pro page only when the owner has set the payment links.

## 10. Privacy in plain words

- **No tracking cookies, no ads.**
- Your **city and weather** are worked out from your connection while the page loads and are not stored.
- **In your own browser:** bookmarks, notepad, quiz progress, flashcards, streak, theme, text size, game scores. Clearing your browser data clears them, unless you are logged in and they sync.
- **Subscribers:** your address, choices, and which edition we sent you on which day.
- **Accounts:** username, a scrambled password (never the password), scrambled recovery codes, your optional email, and the synced items.
- **Services involved:** Vercel and Render (hosting), Cloudflare (speed), Neon (database), Brevo (email), Razorpay (payments), Google (sign-in, if used), Gemini (writes summaries from public articles, never sees reader data).
- **To remove your data:** Manage → Delete my data; or Account → Sign-in and security → delete account; or write to the address on the Contact page.

## 11. Honest limits

- Summaries, "why it matters", background, quiz questions, flashcards and the exam lens are **AI-written and can be wrong**.
- **Importance scores** are the AI's judgement.
- **"Same event" grouping** compares headlines; it can miss an event told in very different words or occasionally merge two similar events.
- **Week ahead dates** come from articles and can be wrong or move.
- The site **refreshes about every six hours**, not live. Some pages (Week ahead, Today in history, extras on a story) fill only after the next refresh and may look empty at first.
- A publisher can ask to be removed; the Sources page says how.

## 12. A 20-minute walk through the site

1. **Home.** Skim the front page; click the lead story.
2. On the **story page**: read *Why it matters*, try **A+ / A−**, press **Read aloud**, scroll to *Also covered by*.
3. Back home, scroll to **More top stories** and press **Detailed**.

## 14. Small glossary

- **Edition:** a set of sections: the UPSC edition (India, World, UPSC, Editorials) or the Engineering edition (Tech, AI).
- **Token:** the long secret part of an address (`?token=…`) that proves a link is yours. Do not share such links.
- **Gist:** the short bullet summary of a story.
- **Exam lens:** the UPSC study notes shown on top stories.
- **Prelims, Mains, Essay, Ethics:** the stages and papers of the UPSC exam.
- **Spaced repetition:** a question card shown sooner if you got it wrong and later if you got it right.
- **Webhook:** an address a Slack or Discord channel gives out so another program can post to it.
- **Sync:** keeping the same bookmarks and progress on all your devices through your account.
- **Refresh / crawl:** the program visiting publishers' pages to collect new stories.
